# English quizzes

Discover and verify the user's quiz destinations, current daily, weekly and full-Memo rules, and original source pages through the available Notion connector. Apply configured dates, timing, scoring, source coverage and layout; do not assume another user's schedule or database. If a required rule or source is unavailable, report the gap rather than fabricate it.

For daily or weekly scored quizzes, map questions to sources, preserve learner answers, and validate unique IDs, rubric, points, timing and coverage before publication. Freeze the answer key and rubric separately without exposing answers in the paper before submission. Reuse an existing paper and never overwrite attempts. Grade only after submission; accept natural equivalents, distinguish required corrections from optional upgrades, and preserve the original. A sampled result does not establish complete mastery.

For full-Memo practice, identify the source by its verified page identity, cover accessible material and state gaps. Preserve prior attempts and feedback, and follow the user's current scoring and timing rules. Link the source and quiz as appropriate without duplicate backlinks. Text or ASR alone cannot support pronunciation or fluency assessment; text-based exercises are not an audio listening test. Read back every affected page and report failures.

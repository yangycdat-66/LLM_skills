# English assessments

Use the Notion connector only; preserve the user's Notion-only assessment workflow, without browser/app workarounds or new local quiz artifacts. Discover and fetch current test center, separate daily/weekly/full-Memo rules, source Memos, review list and live schemas. Sources and rules must be accessible before claiming coverage. This skill does not create a scheduled trigger.

## Shared contract

Use original learning dates and actual original source content. Keep source-to-question mappings and coverage gaps separately from the learner-facing paper. Reconcile stable IDs before creating; preserve first and later attempts and feedback. A sampled score does not establish day/Memo mastery or complete review tasks. Audio-dependent criteria require assessable audio; transcripts support language exercises, not a listening test or pronunciation score.

For scored papers freeze a separate Notion answer key and item-level rubric before publication. Check question IDs, points, realistic timing and source coverage. Do not reveal answers before submission, grade unsubmitted work or adjust criteria to fit an answer. Use the current source_pending/partial-paper rules when sources are insufficient.

## Modes

| Mode | Identity and scope | Baseline, subject to current live rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily F40 | ET-YYYYMMDD-01; current due/overdue/weak sources by original date | 40 minutes, 100 points: vocabulary 20, corrections 20, back-translation 30, transfer 15, speaking 15 |
| Weekly W120 | WT-YYYYMMDD-01; independently verified weekly window and all readable Memos | 120 minutes, 100 points: vocabulary 24, corrections 18, back-translation 24, transfer 16, speaking 14, cross-Memo application 4 |
| Full-Memo | Full source page ID, not title alone; all important accessible learning items | Untimed, unscored; visible questions with separate collapsible dated first/second/later attempts |

Daily and weekly papers are independent: neither replaces the other. Verify timezone, date/window and trigger separately. Do not copy a user's schedule into a portable default. For weekly work enumerate Memos and maintain a per-Memo coverage matrix; incomplete source coverage is not full coverage. For full-Memo work preserve index and source backlinks, append questions when sources change and retain attempts; unavailable/empty sources stay pending.

## Mark and reinforce

Grade only actual submissions with the frozen rubric; accept natural equivalents and distinguish required correction from optional refinement. Keep feedback immediately associated with the preserved answer. Mark unsupported audio criteria unassessed and adjust denominators only per current rules. Qualify provisional observations.

For scored tests create/reuse the authorized concise reinforcement child from real errors and worthwhile expressions, preserving its placement and answers; do not create unrelated vocabulary or sentence archives. Full-Memo practice gets qualitative feedback. Link results to source-date evidence for future priorities, without changing learner-owned completion or mastery.

Read back paper, rubric, attempts, reinforcement, source links and index as applicable. On ambiguous writes inspect identities before retrying. Report completed and pending stages, coverage limits and actual results; never fabricate missing source material, keys or historical scores.
